My approach

How I work

Start by listening

I ask questions and try to understand people's circumstances before deciding what needs to change.

Be clear about the evidence

I distinguish what I observed, how I interpreted it, and what still needs testing. I want others to be able to follow the reasoning.

Keep learning

Engineering, design, and data give me different ways to investigate a problem. I keep building on those foundations as the work calls for it.

Make the work useful

I value clear interfaces, practical documentation, and accessible explanations that help people find their way and contribute.
