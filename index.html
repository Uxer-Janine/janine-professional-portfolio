People × Systems × Data

Hi, I'm Janine.

I'm a service and product designer with an engineering background and a growing specialization in data science.

I work on problems where people, technology, and systems meet. From improving public services to researching how students learn, I enjoy understanding what people need, identifying what isn't working, and finding practical ways to make things better.

My philosophy

“I design with people. Build with data.”

Design helps me understand people's experiences, needs, and frustrations. Data helps me investigate patterns, test assumptions, and understand whether a solution is working.

I pursued both because I believe the strongest solutions need human understanding and measurable evidence.
