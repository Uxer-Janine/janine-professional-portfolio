Personal Roots · Kuria, Kenya

Dismantling barriers and keeping girls in school

I was born and raised in Kuria, Kenya—a community where Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), early forced marriage, and educational dropouts are daily realities. Early on, I committed to standing against these practices. Today, I combine community advocacy with service design and field data—helping organizations track interventions, improve digital systems, and provide girls with safe, sustainable pathways to education.

Active Project · Kuria

ChildsLife Kenya — System Revamp

Redesigning the reporting and outreach tools used by field officers in Kuria to record student attendance, track at-risk girls, and produce donor reports measuring anti-FGM impact.
